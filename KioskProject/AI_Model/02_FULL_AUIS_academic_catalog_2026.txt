AUIS Academic Programs Overview 
The American University of Iraq, Sulaimani (AUIS) offers a tiered educational structure ranging 
from
 
preparatory
 
English

1. Academic Preparatory Program (APP) 
The APP is a U.S.-accredited intensive program designed to prepare students for 
English-medium
 
undergraduate

● Structure: Three 11-week terms per year with four levels of instruction (Foundations 
through
 
Level
 
3).
 ● Instruction: Small classes led by native or near-native speakers, focusing on reading, 
writing,
 
and
 
math.
 ● Grading: Mastery is measured via grades, oral participation, attendance, and 
extracurricular
 
involvement.
 ● Passing Requirements: Students must earn at least 70% in each class to advance. If a 
course
 
is
 
failed,
 
only
 
that
 
specific
 
course
 
must
 
be
 
repeated.
 ● Graduation: Upon completion, students receive a diploma and are eligible for 
undergraduate
 
entry.
 
2. Undergraduate Program 
● Degrees: Bachelor of Arts (BA) or Bachelor of Science (BS). ● Schedule: Two main semesters (Fall/Spring) with optional Winter (January) and 
Summer
 
terms.
 ● Minors: Available by completing specific elective requirements. ● Concurrent Enrollment: Students in the bridge program (R100 and W100) earn 3 
credits
 
per
 
class.
 
Failing
 
these
 
impacts
 
GPA
 
and
 
future
 
core
 
course
 
eligibility.
 
3. Graduation Requirements (APP to Undergraduate) 
To transition into the undergraduate program, students must meet one of the following: 
1. Pass Level 3 (minimum 70% in each class). 2. Complete the Concurrent Enrollment program (Reading 100 and Writing 100). 3. Score a 72 on the TOEFL iBT exam.
